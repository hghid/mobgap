Recordings are processed separately for each calendar day, split at midnight on the device clock. CSV columns: samples, acc_x, acc_y, acc_z, gyr_x, gyr_y, gyr_z. Both formats require the Mobilise-D aligned lower-back sensor frame. No sample-count cap. CSV: up to 64 MiB. CWA: no file-size cap.
